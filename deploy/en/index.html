FAQ

Everything you need to know

We deliver and pick up in Rio’s South Zone and Downtown: Copacabana, Ipanema, Leblon, Botafogo, Flamengo, Leme, Urca, Gávea, Jardim Botânico, Lapa and Santa Teresa. We also serve Barra da Tijuca, Niterói and SDU and GIG airports at their own rates. Need another area? Message us on WhatsApp.
The price depends on where you’re staying: · South Zone and Downtown (Copacabana, Ipanema, Leblon, Botafogo and nearby): R$50 · Santos Dumont Airport (SDU): R$80 · Barra da Tijuca: R$120 · Niterói: R$120 · Galeão International Airport (GIG): R$200 The fee covers delivery AND pickup — no matter how many items you rent.
The minimum is 3 days and you can book up to 21 days online. The more days, the lower the price per day. For more than 21 days, message us on WhatsApp.
